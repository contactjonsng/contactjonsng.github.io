Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Hermes is a private personal assistant used only by its owner, Jon Sng. No one else can sign in to it.

What it accesses

Only with the owner's own sign-in, and read-only: his Gmail messages, his Google Sheets and his Google Drive files.

How the data is used

The data is used only to keep the owner's private notes on his own server. It is not sold, shared with anyone, or used for advertising. Hermes never sends email or changes or deletes files.

Storage and removal

Sign-in tokens are kept on the owner's private server. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google user data

Hermes's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
